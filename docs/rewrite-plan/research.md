## Mechanics and implementation implications

- **Roads and parcels.** The reference offers road classes, curves, upgrades, bridges, intersections, parking and dedicated transit infrastructure. Vibe Town should prove curved roads, junction splitting and durable road-frontage parcels before adding road variants. [Road tools](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/road-tools)
- **Travel and dispatch.** Routes account for travel time, monetary cost and preferences. Services also choose vehicles by route cost. Start with measured travel times, capacity, queues and invalidation when roads change. Add cost and mode choice once those behaviors are observable. [Traffic AI](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/traffic-ai)
- **Households, businesses and trade.** Firms buy inputs, pay expenses and sell products. Households earn income, pay rent and buy goods. The original diary describes subsidies that Economy 2.0 subsequently removed. Vibe Town should design its own opening finances and prove one accounted producer-to-shop-to-household chain. [Economy and production](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/economy-production) · [Economy 2.0](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/news/economy-patch-is-here)
- **Land and services.** The reference combines unlockable land, local coverage, dispatch capacity and district policies. Keep ownership, render chunks and parcels independent. Test whether a real vehicle can reach a request and whether its facility can handle the load. [Maps and themes](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/maps-themes) · [Services and districts](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/city-services-districts-policies)
- **Transit and freight.** Stops and depots support lines with observable usage. Cargo terminals hold inventory for onward trips. Prove bus queues and passenger mode shifts, then add freight transfers using the same trip and inventory rules. [Public and cargo transport](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/public-cargo-transportation)

## Dependency research

- **Three.js** supplies instance batching, camera and lighting infrastructure, and glTF ingestion. The regional spike should compare its WebGL2 renderer with WebGPURenderer on the same map, hardware, camera route and instance counts. [Three.js API](https://threejs.org/docs/) · [WebGLRenderer](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGLRenderer) · [WebGPURenderer and WebGL2 fallback](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/webgpurenderer)
- **React** fits inspectors and structured controls. Its external-store API can subscribe to cached immutable snapshots from the worker. Keep camera motion and instance transforms in the viewport. [React external-store documentation](https://react.dev/reference/react/useSyncExternalStore)
- **React Three Fiber** supports imperative updates and instancing, but adds a scene lifecycle alongside the worker and renderer lifecycles. Defer it during the regional spike. [R3F performance guidance](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/pitfalls)

## Vibe Town baseline

The starting revision is [d540699](https://github.com/deadronos/vibe-town-sim/commit/d540699). Keep the connected-district scenario and save-protection behavior as regression references. The current game uses a 38 × 38 tile grid, road-component utilities, shortest-path commute matching, monthly aggregate finances and a custom WebGL2 renderer. See the [prior repository review](https://github.com/deadronos/vibe-town-sim/issues/1) for its measured baseline and test conditions. The rewrite’s regional numbers are targets to benchmark.
